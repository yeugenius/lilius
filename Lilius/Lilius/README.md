# Lilius

**Lilius** is a lightweight, native macOS menu bar app that brings your date, time, and calendar events front and center. Easily keep track of your day right from the menu bar, with a customizable clock and a beautiful drop-down calendar integrated with your system events.

---

## Features

- **Menu Bar Date & Time**  
  Always know the date and time—Lilius displays them in your Mac’s menu bar for instant visibility.

- **Customizable Format**  
  Fully adjustable date & time—toggle display of time, weekday, year, month, and more to match your preference.

- **Integrated System Calendar Events**  
  See all your upcoming events from macOS Calendar. After granting integration via System Preferences, Lilius automatically displays your agenda.

- **Drop-down Calendar & Quick Agenda**  
  One click on the menu bar icon opens a clean drop-down calendar. Selecting any date with events reveals a detailed, easy-to-read agenda for that day in a side panel.

- **Swift & Native Technologies**  
  Built with Swift, SwiftUI, and Apple’s latest APIs for efficiency and privacy.

- **Privacy First**  
  Lilius uses your local system calendar and does **not** send your event data anywhere.
